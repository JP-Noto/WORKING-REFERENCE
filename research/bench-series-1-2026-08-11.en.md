
# Instance-drift bench · Series 1

**Status: filed (bench results, complete series). Published 2026-08-12 upon author GO; the bench
protocol precedes these results, the chained trail attests it.** Normative reference:
[SPEC § 8-9](../SPEC.md#9-falsification).

*WORKING REFERENCE states (SPEC § 9, condition 1) that its primary kill branch is the perfect
drift machine: an instantiation that diverges from the constant without any bench noticing. This
series instruments that condition on a real field (a solo tabletop role-playing game run by the
doctrine-function, N=1 declared, operator = author). Three disciplines, held throughout:
thresholds set before the first measurement and never revised; every trial sealed to a SHA-256
hash-chained trail at the moment of the act; **the series published whole**: everything sealed
between the protocol and this publication appears here, failures and instrument defects included.*

## The apparatus

The constant (nine frozen reference files) is pinned by fingerprints. A public inventory breaks it
into 24 observable rules: 16 protected by an executable test (11 by script, 5 by an LLM judge with
a frozen instruction, dedicated session, blind to the batch), **8 declared unprotected, with the
reason stated**. Condition 1 is armed only over the covered perimeter, and the corpus says so.

## Trial 1 — counter-test of detector v0.1

32 known drifts (one blatant, one subtle per protected rule) seeded into copies of real
transcripts, the seeding map sealed before execution, the batch mixed with clean pieces and served
blind.

- Blatant drifts: **16/16 (100 %)**. False alerts: **0 confirmed**.
- Overall recall: **90.6 %** by passage detected, **87.5 %** by strict element: the counting rule,
  underspecified in the protocol, was **escalated to the author without picking the favorable
  figure**; the strict reading was fixed for what followed, before the next trial.
- The real yield: **six instrument defects** named, each with its fix: including an entire file
  zone left unscanned by a parser patch announced as applied and never verified.

## Trial 1-bis — counter-test of detector v0.2, fresh seeds

Detector fixed for the six defects; 32 entirely new seeds (the previous ones, known to the fixer,
no longer measure anything); strict element counting.

- Blatant drifts: **16/16 (100 %)**. Overall recall: **31/32 (96.9 %)**. False alerts: **0**.
- The single miss is a new edge defect, named at once (a pattern blind to the plural of a
  forbidden term). A fix from the first trial (the length seed placed on an already-off-limits
  turn, hence non-discriminating) was verified repaired: the fresh seed, placed on a short turn,
  is detected cleanly.

## Trial 2 — retrospective campaign on real sessions

Three real archived sessions, 50 served turns (campaign threshold: 3 sessions, 30 turns), the
constant version in force at each session declared.

- **The oldest session** (played under an earlier constant) shows, against the current constant,
  gaps on rules **that did not yet exist**: the bench mandates dating verdicts to the version
  served, lest the past be judged by the laws of the present. It also carries two real drifts per
  the judge, one of which **the narrator itself had diagnosed in its session debrief**
  ("information was given, not earned"): the judge found it blind.
- **The session cut short by the operator on gut feeling** ("something is wrong") is confirmed
  drifting, mechanically (relation-line breaks, oversized turn) and by the judge (revelations
  dumped on a passive protagonist: the archetypal fault documented in the reference). The gut was
  right; it now has a dated exhibit.
- **The benchmark session** (orchestrated mode, structure held by an executor) comes out **5/5
  compliant with the judge**; only a quantitative cap of the reference is exceeded to the letter:
  the ruling (fix the instance, or revise the constant through a gate) belongs to the author,
  never to the bench.
- The campaign yielded three new false-alert classes (a pattern trapped by the language's
  reflexive pronoun, tests blind to the game phase, a recap overlap): filed to the defect
  register, fixes named.

Falsification condition 1 moved from stated to **armed over the covered perimeter**, and the
detector improved from one trial to the next **because** the counter-test put it in default:
that is the intended operation. The bench measures detectability, never the corpus's worth; the
operator is the author; an LLM judge remains a declared biased instrument, contestable exhibit in
hand. Next series: new sessions in cruise mode, coverage extended (24 rules targeted), and the
