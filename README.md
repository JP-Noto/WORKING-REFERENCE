# WORKING REFERENCE

**The reference that works: a corpus is not a document you read: it is a function you call.**

> **Rank of the work presented here: hypothesis : nothing more.**

This repository presents WORKING REFERENCE: its thesis, the problem it studies, and its method.
**The French documents are authoritative** : this page is an orientation for English readers, not a
translation ([entrée française](README.fr.md)).

## What it studies

Reference corpora fail in two symmetric ways once work has duration: bulk-loaded, their cost grows
with their size; consulted on demand, what reaches the work depends on memory or similarity, and
nobody detects the drift between the work and the reference.

WORKING REFERENCE claims a corpus is a **function**: a **constant** (the invariant earned through
explicit gates, what must survive a model-generation change) plus **variables**, instantiated by the
**reference layer** from the declared state of the work in progress, **served** (never bulk-loaded)
and **sealed** (every serving decision is traceable and chained). Medium is a variable: one more
medium costs a generator and a bench, never a second reference layer. The measurable property: the
volume served per call does not grow with corpus size.

Gates, benches and ranks are **roles**, not institutions: a conforming deployment holds them with its
own proof apparatus, and names it.

Five layers, no redundancy: a host AI OS governs the system; [LIVING
REFERENCE](https://github.com/JP-Noto/LIVING-REFERENCE) governs the status of knowledge; WORKING
REFERENCE governs how the reference serves the work; [MYSTANCE](https://github.com/JP-Noto/MYSTANCE)
governs the human's place; [SOUNDNESS](https://github.com/JP-Noto/SOUNDNESS) governs the birth of
document-extracted knowledge. The family is operated by the ONDE AI R&D laboratory — its portal:
<https://github.com/JP-Noto/ONDE>.
